# Notice

This public package supports scientific replication of the Kincade vineyard
wildfire resilience study. It is an open screening / evidence package, not an
operational fire-behavior model, insurance score, or smoke-taint diagnosis.

- dNBR is a spectral response indicator, not field-calibrated vine damage.
- HMS smoke polygons indicate potential overhead exposure, not PM2.5 dose or grape taint.
- Road topology is geometry only (no traffic, capacity, or closures).
- Recovery metrics are greenness ratios, not yield or wine-quality outcomes.
